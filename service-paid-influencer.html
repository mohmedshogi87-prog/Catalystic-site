Campaigns

Paid reach, spent where it can actually be measured

Advertising works well for intake deadlines, launches and openings. It works badly as a permanent substitute for having something worth finding.

Campaigns with an end date and a number

An admissions intake, a project launch, an opening. We set the target before the spend, and if the campaign is not reaching it we say so rather than quietly renewing.

Creator partnerships, matched properly

A creator with fifteen thousand engaged followers in your city is usually worth more than one with half a million spread across a continent. We match on audience overlap, not follower count.

Tracking that survives the campaign

Conversion tracking, UTM discipline and a reporting sheet you keep. Most firms cannot tell which channel produced last year's enquiries, which makes every budget conversation a guess.

  • Ad creative, static and video

Common questions

What is a sensible starting budget?

It depends on the market and the goal, but a campaign too small to gather data is money spent learning nothing. We will tell you the floor for your objective before you commit.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

No. A flat management fee, so our incentive is not to talk you into spending more.

Can you run campaigns in Arabic?

Yes, with creative written in Arabic and targeting built per country. Gulf markets differ enough from each other that one regional campaign usually underperforms three local ones.
