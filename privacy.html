Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Catalystic collects through catalystic.my, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. Catalystic operates from Seri Kembangan, Selangor, Malaysia.

What we collect

  • Your name, company, email address and phone number, when you complete the enquiry form or contact us by email or WhatsApp.
  • The content of your message, including which service you are interested in.
  • Whether you opted in to receive occasional marketing emails.

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies on this site. If we add analytics later, this page will be updated before it goes live.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal.
  • To deliver work you have engaged us for, and to invoice for it.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you asked for them.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 24 months. Records relating to work we carried out are kept for seven years, because Malaysian tax and accounting rules require it. Marketing subscribers are kept until they unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Write to info@catalystic.my and we will respond within 21 days, as required under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
