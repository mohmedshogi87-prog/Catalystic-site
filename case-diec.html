Engineering · Oman

Dhofar Integrated Engineering Company

A complete online and printed identity for an Omani engineering contractor, delivered in Arabic and English and self-hosted on the client's own infrastructure.

The situation

DIEC is an established engineering contractor in Dhofar with a serious project record and almost no way for a prospective client or a procurement officer to verify it. There was no functioning website, no consistent identity across documents, and no company profile that could be attached to a tender submission. In a sector where credibility is checked before a call is ever made, that gap was costing them at the shortlist stage.

What we did

  • Designed and iterated the website across several directions before settling on one that suited a technical audience rather than a consumer one
  • Built the site in multiple formats so the client was not locked into one platform: a static HTML build, a WordPress theme package, and a Webflow guide
  • Deployed to the client's own cPanel hosting at their own domain, with every credential handed over
  • Produced a brand identity guide in Arabic, so the people using it day to day could actually read it
  • Delivered a company profile in both PDF and PowerPoint, plus business cards
  • Produced an organisation chart for use in tenders and client presentations

Where it landed

DIEC now has a single consistent identity across its website, tender documents and printed material, hosted on infrastructure it controls. The Arabic-first brand guide meant the standard was actually adopted internally rather than filed and forgotten, which is the usual fate of an English guide in an Arabic-speaking office.
