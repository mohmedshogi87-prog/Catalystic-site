A small studio, on purpose

Run from Seri Kembangan, Selangor. The person you brief is the person who builds.

Catalystic works with firms selling something where trust decides the sale: a school place, an engineering contract, a supplement someone puts in their body. That kind of buyer researches before they call. Our job is to make sure that when they research you, they find something that holds up.

Work is done by a small team rather than passed down a chain of account managers. That is a real constraint, and it means we take on fewer projects than a larger agency would. It also means nothing gets lost between the brief and the build, which for firms of the size we work with is usually worth more than scale.

An engineering background, used deliberately

The founder trained as a construction management engineer at IUKL before moving into marketing, and has spent more than eight years in marketing since. That matters in two practical ways. Technical clients do not have to simplify anything to be understood, and the claims that end up on your website are ones we have actually checked rather than ones that merely sound impressive.

Three sectors

Education, engineering and health and wellness e-commerce. Each has a buyer who decides differently. Parents choose on trust and reassurance, over months. Procurement chooses on documented evidence of delivery. An online buyer decides in ninety seconds and will not come back if the page is slow. Knowing that in advance means we are not learning your market at your expense.

Two languages

We work in English and Arabic, and we build Arabic properly: right-to-left at the layout level, with Arabic typography and Arabic copy, not an English page run through a plugin. Roughly half our client work sits in the Gulf and East Africa, so this is a working requirement rather than a marketing claim.

Three markets

Malaysia, the Gulf and East Africa. Calls run to your time zone. Gulf clients can book into the evening, Malaysia time.
